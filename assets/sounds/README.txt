Put your own emotional sound recordings here (optional).

Name a file after the sound:  laugh.mp3, giggle.mp3, cry.mp3, angry.mp3, frustrated.mp3,
sigh.mp3, gasp.mp3, scream.mp3, yawn.mp3, hmm.mp3  (wav, mp3, ogg, m4a, flac, aac and opus work).

To use different recordings for female and male characters, add the word female or male:
laugh_female.mp3 and laugh_male.mp3.

Your file is used instead of the built-in synthesized sound. The first 10 seconds are used.
Restart the app after adding or changing files.
