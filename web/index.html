Shorts Studio

Your story

Describe what happens in a few sentences. Hindi or English both work.

Cast

Name each character, pick a look and a voice, and play a sample to hear it.

Scenes and sound

Choose where the sound comes from, then write or draft your scenes. In each scene you choose which characters are in it, and with your own audio you choose which part of it the scene plays.

Sound

"Write captions from my audio" listens to your audio with Gemini and types each line's words (Hindi in Devanagari). With no lines yet, it makes one line per spoken part. Lines you leave empty are also captioned automatically when you make the video.

Your audio is the soundtrack and the characters act while it plays. Each scene plays its own trimmed part of it, so you can use one long recording for the whole video. The text of a line becomes the caption and can stay empty.

Background music (optional)

Or use your own music file below; your file wins when both are set.

Every line can also have an optional emotional sound (laugh, angry growl, frustrated groan, and more). A line with a sound and no words is a reaction: that character stays in the scene and reacts while the sound plays. Leave it on "No sound" if you do not want one.

Voices

Generate the audio for every line. The length of each clip sets how long its scene lasts.

Gemini voices speak each line with its emotion (angry, crying, laughing...). Pick a "Gemini" voice for a character in the Cast step, or choose "Gemini voices for everyone" here. The free Gemini tier has a small daily limit; when it runs out, lines use the Microsoft voices. Each line is made once and saved, so changing one line only remakes that line.

You chose your own audio in the Scenes and sound step, so there are no voices to make. Go on to make the video.

Make the video

Start with a quick preview. When it looks right, make the full-quality version and upload it to YouTube yourself.

Flat cartoon scenes (a "Flat cartoon" background, or any "Flat:" character) are drawn in a simple 2D street-cartoon style with an automatic camera: wide shots, shots on the speaker, over-the-shoulder shots and close-ups. Pick a shot per line in the Scenes step to override it.
